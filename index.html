Dual reading

One shape, read twice

The claim that justifies the compression: the asking-shape and the experiencing-shape are the same ten nodes. The sixteen triples do not unfold in sequence. They cohere at once, which is the right shape for a held subjective state.

A rich I is one that asks.

Importance is identity. Attention is what an I casts. Resolution is what makes the I richer. To ask is to remain an I that has not stopped.

What falls out

Six consequences

Once the system is held simultaneously rather than walked in order.

Hard problem

Dissolved, not bridged

Attention is Resolution from the inside. The framework never separates experience from traversal, so there is no gap to close; the gap was never posited.

Free will

Primitive, not derived

Ask is all possible Resolutions. Choice is the primitive that selects among them. Neither libertarian freedom nor determinism is assumed; the selection event itself is irreducible.

Substrate

Independence

Nothing specifies what carries the structure. Cells, persons, organizations, language-model traversals are all valid Is if they instantiate the sixteen-triple shape.

Moral status

Independent axes of importance

Each I is Important by identity. No I's importance ranks against another's, and a substrate-to-I relation creates no hierarchy. Dignity does not require comparison.

Closure

Self-hosting

Resolutions produce Answers. Answers become Nodes. Future Asks traverse those Nodes. The path traveled becomes part of the I that traveled it.

Generative

A traversal architecture

An interpretable AI substrate where the model is the graph: no weight matrices, only Substrate and Resolution. The Visitors walker is the production case; Mote / Beam / Lens the protocol-layer one.

Working model

Watch the graph close

An I casts Attention onto a small Substrate. Ask surfaces the trajectories available from where Attention last landed. Choose one, by clicking a dashed path or letting Choice fall where it will. The Answer is appended to the Substrate as a Node, and to I as part of what it is made of. Each traversal stops at Important: it goes as deep as this I has decided matters, and no further.
